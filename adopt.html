Simple & thoughtful

How to Adopt

We don't issue blanket approvals — every application is reviewed against the specific needs of the dog you're interested in. Here's the full journey from hello to homecoming.

1

Submit Your Application

Tell us about your home and your hopes. Home photos are required (a short video works too). The more detail you share, the better the match.

2

Review & Approval

If approved, we'll email you to connect you with the dog's current foster so you can schedule a meet and greet within two weeks.

3

Meet & Confirm

After meeting the dog, you'll have 24 hours to confirm. If you move forward, we email you an adoption contract to sign.

4

Welcome Home

Once the signed agreement and adoption fee are returned, you'll coordinate pick-up directly with the foster. Your new best friend comes home.

Step one, in detail

What we look for in an application

We don't issue blanket approvals. If you don't have a particular dog in mind, simply note that on your application and we'll help you find your match.

Please don't apply if you're unsure, or if you aren't ready to schedule a meet and greet within two weeks of applying. Thoughtful applications help us make the right match the first time.

Application requirements

  • Home photos are required for all applicants — a short video may be submitted in their place.
  • Detail matters. The more you tell us about your home, schedule, and other pets, the better we can match you.
  • Be ready to meet. Only apply if you can schedule a meet and greet within two weeks.
  • 24-hour confirmation after meeting — otherwise we schedule meets with other interested applicants.

Good to know

Policies & special cases

A few important notes before you apply, so there are no surprises along the way.

🐶 Puppy care (under 12 weeks)

Puppies under 12 weeks require a care plan with your application. Young puppies shouldn't be left alone longer than 2–3 hours at a time, so please tell us how you plan to care for one day to day.

🗺️ Out-of-state adopters

We're a New York City rescue and prioritize placements within our region. Out-of-state applications are considered case by case — reach out before applying so we can talk through travel and the home-check details.

✉️ Questions first?

Not sure if you're ready, or have a question about a specific dog? Email our adoption team at Adopt.TrueNorthRescue@gmail.com and we'll help.
