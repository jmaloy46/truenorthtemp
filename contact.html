A quick note

No phone line, no front door — and that's by design

True North Rescue Mission has no public phone number or physical address. We're entirely volunteer-run and 100% foster-based: every dog lives in a loving foster home, not a shelter. Email lets our volunteers respond thoughtfully around their day jobs and the dogs in their care.
